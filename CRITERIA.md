# Telegraph Hackathon Season II: judging criteria

Source: https://anjoman.ai/dashboard/hackathon/season-2/rules (read 15 Nov 2026; re-check before submitting, the organizers update it).
Deadline: 1 Dec 2026, 23:59 UTC (2 Dec, 00:59 Lagos).

## To win
- At least 70/100, AND at least 24/35 on technical quality, AND a working Telegraph purchase.
- Only entries that meet "Every entry must show" are scored.

## Every entry must show
1. Something working (app, callable Provider, or valid Evaluator contribution).
2. A real Telegraph integration, with genuine evidence for every claim.
3. Repeatable results: instructions for new inputs and error cases.
4. Safe handling: permissions, spending limits, error handling, no leaked keys or private data.
5. Accurate claims: say which network; testnet is not production.
6. The bounties repo starred on GitHub.

Apps & Agents also: at least one genuinely autonomous machine-to-machine purchase
through the supported Telegraph flow, then use the intelligence for a meaningful task.

## Scoring: Apps & Agents track (100 points)
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Technical quality and mainnet readiness (minimum 24) | 35 |
| Commercial viability (clear user, real problem, useful result, sensible costs, believable adoption path) | 25 |
| Organic growth, education, developer activation | 20 |
| Genuine independent adoption and retention | 10 |
| Telegraph integration and usage quality | 5 |
| Reproducibility, demo/tutorial, verifiable proof | 5 |

## Scoring: Miners/Providers track
Not yet captured (Section 6, Miners / Providers tab).

### The 20 growth points, itemised (same in every track)
- Content and education (5): public explanation or demo (2); technical tutorial explaining Telegraph's role (2); honest results, limits, lessons (1).
- Ease of adoption (5): steps with prerequisites and an example (2); working demo/repo/endpoint others can test (2); troubleshooting, costs, next steps (1).
- Organic distribution (5): 3+ original updates on separate dates (2); shared beyond own team/Discord thread (2); real technical discussion or follow-up (1).
- Community activation (5): a specific invitation with a next step (1); evidence an independent developer acted (2); a further independent contribution, integration or repeat use (2).
- Not counted: stars, likes, follows, reposts, joins. Prohibited: bought or traded engagement, fake adoption.

## Mission acceptance (a reviewer must be able to)
1. Run the app with a new, realistic input.
2. Check the useful result.
3. Inspect the real Telegraph request and its transaction/accounting evidence.
4. Test a reasonable failure case.
5. Reproduce the result from our instructions.

## Five questions every app answers
1. Who is the user or buyer, and how often do they have this problem?
2. What does it improve?
3. What does it do today?
4. Why would someone adopt it?
5. How can someone try it?

## Submission contents
1. Project details (track, missions/Intents, working link, GitHub username that starred the repo).
2. Demo, 2-5 minutes: problem, Telegraph request, intelligence and Provider evidence, observed cost, result, how to try it.
3. How to run it (beginner-friendly, sample request, expected result, network, costs, known limits).
4. Evidence (receipts, requests, accounting, endpoints, tests, rankings).
5. Short public summary with links to updates, tutorial and independent testing.
6. Showcase permission.

## Prizes
- Apps & Agents: 8 delivery awards x $225; excellence $700 / $350 / $150.
- Miners/Providers: 4 delivery x $300; excellence $500 / $300.
- Evaluators: 4 delivery x $300; excellence $500 / $300.
- December pool: 4 x $500 (new mission results, technical work); 2 x $500 (real adoption).
- App delivery awards favour: most distinct completed missions, then most distinct apps,
  then missions served by only one entrant.
