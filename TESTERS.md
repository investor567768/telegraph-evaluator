## Tester 1: Fexx (OathCast), independent developer
- **What he did:** ran the live seller-check page 4 times on 18 Nov (about 11:41 to 11:50 UTC) on Ethereum and Base, then read the code. Posted results and screenshots as [issue #1 on this repository](https://github.com/investor567768/telegraph-evaluator/issues/1) ([screenshots](https://github.com/investor567768/telegraph-evaluator/issues/1#issuecomment-6977800130)).
- **What he found:** two correct verdicts, one correct refusal on a wrong chain, and a real bug: a seller answer typed as "75.07M" was read as $75.07. Plus four suggestions (show the gap between the two sources, per-source times, a zero reserve divides by zero, friendly message on HTTP 429).
- **What changed:** all of it fixed the same day in commit `1fd64a1`, which closed the issue. The page now reads k / m / b amounts, refuses anything it cannot read, shows the amount it read, gives no verdict when the two sources disagree by more than 2%, and shows each source's reply time. Live check after the fix: "75.07M" read as $75,070,000.00, within 2% of both sources ([screenshot](seller-check-v2-75.07M.png)).
- **Repeat use:** Fexx re-ran the live page on 18 Nov at 12:23 UTC and confirmed both fixes on the same pool: "75.07M" read as $75,070,000.00, DexScreener −0.07%, GeckoTerminal −0.09%, within 2% of both (comment on [issue #1](https://github.com/investor567768/telegraph-evaluator/issues/1)).
- Fexx is himself an H2 entrant on the Miners and Evaluators tracks.

## Tester 3: antikunpiece (Praman Desk), independent developer
- **What he did:** tested the live site on 28 Nov (about 11:37 UTC) and filed three reproducible findings as [issue #3 on this repository](https://github.com/investor567768/telegraph-evaluator/issues/3), with the exact requests, the actual answers and what he expected. Posted a summary in the Telegraph Discord #hackathon channel.
- **What he found:** (1) the weather source refused a day that had already ended at the place (Auckland, 28 Nov) because it compared the UTC date; (2) city names shared by several places (Springfield, Paris, Boston) were resolved to one place without saying so; (3) the delivery check accepted and released payment for a report with an invented CEO, because the register cannot confirm that field.
- **What changed:** all three fixed the same day. The weather cutoff now uses the place's own time zone (this also stops places behind UTC returning an unfinished day). Ambiguous names return the place used plus the other candidates. A report that states a fact the register cannot confirm (CEO, tax ID, revenue, owners, employees and similar) is now held (`hold_unchecked`, ACP action "hold") instead of paid. A follow-up change the same afternoon caught keys written as `tax_id`, `taxId` or `employee_count`. Tests were added for each case.
- **In return:** he invited us to test his Provider. We did on 28 Nov and filed four findings on his repository ([issue #2](https://github.com/VishAlgoDev/praman-desk/issues/2)). His CVE lookup matched NIST on both CVEs we compared and his nested-input form worked. This was reciprocal testing between two entrants, disclosed here.
- antikunpiece is himself an H2 entrant (Praman Desk).
